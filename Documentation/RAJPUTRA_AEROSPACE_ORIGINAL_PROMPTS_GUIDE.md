## 🔍 Key Design Architecture:
1. **Airplane Tricycle Landing Gear (Wheels):**
   - **Front Nose Gear:** Ek steerable front nose wheel with sleek vertical strut cockpit chin ke neeche.
   - **Rear Main Gear:** Do wide-stance rear main airplane wheels with wire spokes and black rubber tires, mounted on diagonal aerodynamic carbon-fiber cantilever struts.
2. **Bird-Inspired Aft Pylons & Thrusters:** Rear fuselage Swallow-tailed Kite bird ki tarah aerodynamic swept pylons me failta hai jinke ends par cylindrical black ducted thruster fan nacelles lage hain.
3. **Autogyro Rotor:** Overhead central mast par strictly **single straight horizontal twin-blade (2 blades)** carbon-fiber rotor.
4. **Fuselage:** Metallic copper-orange upper teardrop shell + satin dark carbon-fiber lower hull.
5. **Canopy:** Large panoramic curved tinted bubble canopy with twin cockpit leather seats. Clean cabin flanks (no side-mounted fans).
